v4call — Announce Nostr Identity on Hive

Publish a nostr-announce post to Hive that binds your Nostr npub to your Hive account with a cryptographic proof you control the matching nsec.
nGate scanners read these posts to whitelist users on Nostr relays — the Hive post is the trust root, the signed attestation inside it is the proof.
What this does: you paste your Nostr nsec → the page builds a kind-30078 attestation event with your Hive account in a tag → signs it with the nsec entirely in your browser → embeds it (base64) in a Hive post titled nostr-announce. The nsec never leaves this tab.
Don't have an nsec yet? Generate one in ↺ nostr-gen.html (browser-only, keys never leave your machine).
