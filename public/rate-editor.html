TOKEN RATES
Accept Hive-Engine tokens (PIZZA, HIVE, your own token…) in addition to HBD. Add one section per token. Enter 0 for FREE. Token holders can optionally bypass your blocklist.
RATE LISTS
Named lists of users get custom rates. Lists are checked in order — first match wins. Anyone not matched uses the default rates. Enter 0 in any field to make it FREE.
NOSTR ATTESTATION (required when claiming a Nostr key)
Prove you own the Nostr key you've declared above by signing a NIP-78 (kind 30078) event tying your Nostr identity to your Hive username. The signed event gets embedded (base64-JSON) in your rates post. Without this, user-tier nGate relays reject your post — anyone could claim to own your npub. The attestation is invalidated automatically when you change your Hive username, server, NOSTR NPUB, or NOSTR HEX — re-sign after edits.
